Creative Arts Psychotherapy
(Dramatherapy)

For when talking isn’t enough

I offer creative arts psychotherapy (dramatherapy) for adults, young people and children who feel stuck, overwhelmed or disconnected—especially when words are hard to find.

Dramatherapy uses creativity, the body and imagination to explore experience safely and at your own pace.

How dramatherapy works

Objects & images

Exploring experience through objects, images and props.

Story & metaphor

Using storytelling and imagination to gain new perspectives.

Movement & embodiment

Expressing feelings safely through movement and the body.

Reflection & integration

Time to make sense of what comes up and reflect back.

About Sarah

I’m a HCPC-registered dramatherapist and psychotherapist based in Glasgow, with an MA in Dramatherapy from the University of Roehampton and nine years of experience since qualifying.

I use creative approaches alongside talking therapy to help people explore what’s going on for them in a way that feels supportive—working at a pace that feels right for you, whether that’s with words, objects, story or movement.

  • MA Dramatherapy — University of Roehampton
  • HCPC-registered Drama Therapist
  • Trained in Dyadic Developmental Psychotherapy (Level 1)
  • Trained in MANTRA — Maudsley Anorexia Nervosa Treatment for Adults
  • Works with children, teens and adults — individually

Data Protection

If you have any concerns about how I have collected, used, stored or shared your personal information, please contact me in the first instance using the contact details provided in this Privacy Notice. I take data protection concerns seriously and will investigate your complaint fairly and promptly.

I will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 30 days and keep you informed of the progress of my investigation where appropriate. Once my investigation is complete, I will explain the outcome and any action I have taken.

If you remain dissatisfied with my response, you have the right to raise your concerns with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s independent authority for data protection. Details of how to contact the ICO are available on its website at www.ico.org.uk.
