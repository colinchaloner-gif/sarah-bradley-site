In the Waiting

Southside Counselling and Therapy Centre · 3 Carment Drive, Shawlands, Glasgow G41 3PP

A gentle, creative therapeutic workshop for anyone navigating infertility, fertility treatment, IVF or pregnancy loss. Using images, objects and metaphor drawn from dramatherapy, it offers somewhere to pause and put some of what you’re carrying down for a while — no acting, no pressure to share, and no creative experience needed.

Making Meaning Together: Improvisation, Group Process & Transformative Play

British Psychodrama Association Conference 2026 · The Old Swan Hotel, Harrogate · Co-facilitated with Katie Edkins

An experiential conference workshop inviting participants into the playful, collaborative world of long-form improvisation as a creative tool for re-sourcing the self — exploring how group dynamics, role patterns and relational tensions can be surfaced and transformed through symbolic play, with no performance experience needed.
